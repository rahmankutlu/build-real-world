# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful and specific. Critique ideas and evidence, not people. Welcome corrections. Assume good intent while acknowledging impact. Do not publish private information or use sexualized, insulting, threatening, or discriminatory language.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project owner through the contact method on their GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented, and protect reporter privacy.

This code is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
